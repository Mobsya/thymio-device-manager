# Authors and contributors

The following people contributed directly to the TDM:

* Corentin Jabot <corentin dot jabot at mobsya dot org> (also cor3ntin)
  Original implementation, architecture, core development and bug fixes.

* Michael Bonani <michael dot bonani at mobsya dot org>

* Yves Piguet <yves dot piguet at epfl dot ch>

* Valentina (ValentinaTibs) <tibaldo dot valentina at gmail dot com>

* Davide Poveromo (podak, davidepoveromo) <podak4a at gmail dot com>
  MSVC build compatibility and Boost update.

* Matvey Khokhlov <matvey dot khokhlov at epfl dot ch>

  extraction of the standalone TDM project.
